2. Violations

If you violate these rules, you must expect warnings and/or bans. The bans depend on the severity of the offense and range from a 14-day forum ban to a permanent ban from all VATGER services.

The use of the services is only permitted using your real name. This name is automatically generated from the name given to VATSIM when the account is created. A change of name is also only possible at VATSIM itself. Exceptions to this rule are not possible for VATSIM Germany. Exceptions on the part of VATSIM remain unaffected by this.

Such content in posts, profiles or signatures will be removed without warning if discovered and may result in the member being immediately blocked from VATGER services. Links to YouTube or similar are only permitted if you as the publisher in the VATGER services can ensure that the videos shown there are free of third-party rights. Please also refer to our published copyright guidelines.

5. No deletion of contributions in the event of account deletion

In the event that you wish to delete your account with us, your posts will not be deleted. Only your user name will be anonymized so that a post is no longer directly linked to your name. The content of your posts or the posts of others will remain unaffected by this.

6. Choice of words

Use a moderate choice of words and a good language style. Foul language or similar as well as provocations of others are not permitted. Insults against other members, third parties, institutions or companies, xenophobia and sexism are strictly prohibited. Discussions about politics, religion and ideologies are not permitted.

Advertising for political parties, interest groups and campaigns as well as religious communities, sects or ideological movements is not permitted, especially if commercial, political or missionary intentions are being pursued. Any other form of advertising may be prohibited by those responsible for VATGER services in individual cases.

8. Commercial Offers

In view of the fact that VATSIM is a non-commercial organization, VATGER's online offers are also provided exclusively for non-commercial use. In particular, auctions and commercial services, references or links to these (e.g. eBay) with the intention of making a profit are prohibited in all VATGER online offers. They can be deleted without prior warning. However, offers by VATGER members remain permissible if they are published exclusively within the VATGER online offers and the items or services offered are to be sold largely at cost price. Offers for charitable purposes require the prior approval of the VATGER management.

9. Additional regulations Forum

The provisions in this section apply to the VATGER forum (board.vatsim-germany.org).

9.1. Publishing content

Content published in the forum (including screenshots thereof) is not released for publication outside the forum. Feel free to link to posts instead.

9.2. Use of your name in e.g. CPT banners

Your name may be used on advertising banners for e.g. CPTs, which are only published in the area of the forum accessible to registered users. If you do not want this, please discuss this with your event team in advance.

9.3. One thread - one topic, use the search function

If new topics arise in a thread, open a new thread if necessary. Please also use the forum search function to avoid duplicate threads. Posting the same issue in several threads to attract attention is not permitted.

9.4. Signatures and avatars

Signatures: A maximum of one image is permitted in the signature. The image may not be larger than 400x80 pixels (width x height) and the file may not be larger than 50 kByte. Animated images are permitted as long as the file size does not exceed 50 kByte. Images embedded in the post whose content obviously serves the purpose of a signature are treated in the same way as images in the signature. Signature banners issued by VATSIM are excluded from the limitations. VATSIM Germany provides a version of the VATSIM banner via Rotban that is adapted to the forum system. Its use is recommended, but not mandatory.

9.5 Virtual airlines

Postings in connection with the founding of a VA, advertising a VA or calling for participation in it are only permitted in the area provided for this purpose. In this area, posts are first checked by the staff and are only visible once they have been activated.

9.6. “Events” forum area

New threads in the Events forum area may only be created by members of the VATSIM Germany event teams including FIRs or the event teams of external VACCs (only under “international events”). Threads started by other participants in this area can be deleted or moved without warning. Exceptions to this can be made with the PR&Events team of VATSIM Germany.

10. Additional regulations TeamSpeak

The provisions in this section apply to the VATGER TeamSpeak server (ts.vatsim-germany.org).
Without exception, it is not permitted to stream conversations from Teamspeak or the application in which the users connected to the server can be seen. Recording conversations in Teamspeak is only permitted with the consent of all those being recorded and using the Teamspeak recording function.

Violations of the above rules will be punished as follows:

First violation
The user will receive a warning and TS access will be blocked for 14 days.

Second violation
The user's access to all VATGER services (including the forum) will be blocked for 90 days. Guest pilots may lose their permissions.

Third violation
Permanent blocking of the user from all VATGER services. Guest pilots may lose their guest pilot status.
